## 5. Caption Spec (exact)

- **Case:** all lowercase. No punctuation at all — no periods, no commas, no question marks.
- **Numbers:** written as digits (`at 9`, `3 years`, `30 day`).
- **Position:** horizontally centred, baseline sits at roughly **72–78 %** of frame height (lower third, above the safe-zone UI).
- **Style:** bold geometric sans, white fill, soft dark shadow/outline for legibility.
- **Cadence:** one short phrase per card, swapped **on the beat of the spoken word**. Cards persist across cuts when a single line spans two shots (see Shot 4→5 and 9→10).
- **Hold:** a caption is cleared ~0.3–0.5 s before a purely emotional beat (the crying hold, the exit shot) so the image plays silent.
