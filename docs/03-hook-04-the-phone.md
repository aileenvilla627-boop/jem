## 4. THE STANDARD ENDING — apply to Hooks 1, 2, 3 and 4

Gaston: *"every hook is going to finish the same way. The cheater husband is going to end up
leaving the kitchen, the house."*

Lock these three shots as a **reusable tail** on every hook variant:

```
TAIL-A  0.7 s  WIDE kitchen (the hook's opening framing, reused)
               Husband walks left → right, straight out of frame. No look back.

TAIL-B  1.8 s  WIDE kitchen, same framing, now empty of him
               Wife alone, barefoot, arms down, black suitcase on the floor beside her.
               She does not move. OFF-SCREEN: front door closes.

TAIL-C  0.6 s  Same framing, slow fade to near-black → BODY begins
               Caption in: "the lawyer said the divorce would take"
```

**Rules for the tail (do not break):**
1. **Reuse the hook's own opening wide shot framing** — the audience recognises the return, and the emptiness reads.
2. **He never looks back.** No pause in the doorway, no final line.
3. **She never follows.** No reaching, no shouting after him, no collapse. Stillness only.
4. **The suitcase stays in frame** in every variant — it's the series' visual signature.
5. **The door close is heard, never seen.** That is how "leaving *the house*" is paid off without a new set.
6. **No caption over TAIL-A or TAIL-B.** The silence is the payoff.
