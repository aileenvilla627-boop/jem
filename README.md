## The 10-second version

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 720x1280, 30 fps, Pixar-style 3D, burned-in lowercase captions, a cut roughly every second.
- **Characters:** wife = **pomegranate head**, husband = **dried walnut head**, doctor = **fully human**. Locked — the PDF's avatar images are stills from the reference video.
- **Structure:** one of four interchangeable ~15 s kitchen hooks → shared ~110 s body (collapse → doctor → mechanism → transformation → CTA).
- **Gaston's note:** all four hooks now end the same way — **the husband walks out of the kitchen and out of the house**, she stays still, the suitcase stays on the floor. Spec'd as a reusable 3-shot tail.
- **⚠ Watch:** the reference sells **Lymphoria** (drops, 55% off); our script sells **Tavio** (castor oil pack, 33% off). Copy the craft, not the product — the mechanism and end-card beats must be re-shot.
